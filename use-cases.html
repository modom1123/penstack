USE CASES

Better listings for real store problems.

A new launch needs copy. An older listing needs better details. A growing catalog needs consistency. Start with the job your store needs done.

1

Prepare the next product launch.

The product is ready to meet customers. Retrieve its Shopify draft, explain what makes it useful, and finish the details shoppers will look for.

  • Draft a description from your brief, existing copy, and product metafields.
  • Check features, materials, and care information against the actual product.
  • On paid plans, apply Brand Voice to the draft.
Learn more →
2

Give an older listing a useful refresh.

An old description may say “great for everyday use” without explaining size, materials, or care. Bring in the listing and replace vague language with verified details.

  • Add the supported product details shoppers need to choose.
  • Replace vague claims with specific, accurate explanations.
  • Edit and republish the same product without using another publish allowance.
Learn more →
3

Write for a specific search.

A shopper searching for a “dishwasher-safe ceramic mug” has told you what matters. If your product fits, explain those details clearly. That is search intent in practice.

  • Choose relevant phrases that match the product’s actual qualities.
  • Use accurate titles and readable copy that answer the shopper’s question.
  • Carry that context into the description and supporting product details.
Explore SEO guidance →
4

Put products where shoppers expect them.

One running shoe can belong in Running Shoes, New Arrivals, and Training Essentials. Review the product’s attributes, then assign tags and collections that make sense for your store.

  • Use consistent product attributes across related items.
  • Assign the relevant Shopify collections.
  • Check tags for relevance and duplication.
Learn about product organization →
5

Keep each store’s identity intact.

A skincare store and a snowboard store need different language. On Pro and Agency, keep each store’s Brand Voice, product data, and SEO context separate.

  • Work with the product data belonging to the selected store.
  • Write for each store’s own audience and products.
  • Apply the Brand Voice and search context intended for that store.
