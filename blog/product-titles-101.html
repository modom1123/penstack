Product Titles 101: What Should Actually Go in One?

How to write clearer titles for shoppers and search without turning the title into a keyword storage locker.

pBy the penstack team
September 25, 2026 · 7 min read

A good product title should answer one question fast: what is this?

The next job is to add the detail that helps a shopper tell this item apart from similar options. Anything beyond that has to earn its place.

The short answer

Lead with the product type. Add the most useful distinguishing detail. Leave the rest for the description, variants, metafields, or filters.

In this article

What belongs in a title Why order matters What stays out A simple formula Quick publishing check

1. What actually belongs in a product title?

Start with the product type. Then add the attribute that helps someone identify the right version.

Product type
What the item is: water bottle, running shoe, T-shirt.
Key attribute
What sets it apart: size, fit, capacity, style.
Important qualifier
Use material, color, audience, or use-case only when it helps the decision.
Only what’s useful
Keep every word accurate, specific, and relevant.

2. Order matters

Shoppers usually need to know what the product is before they need material, size, or other details. Put the product type first, then add the most important attribute.

3. What stays out

A product title is not the place to store every keyword, promotion, technical specification, or selling point.

Avoid
Brand X Insulated Water Bottle 32 oz Stainless Steel BPA Free Leak Proof Double Wall Vacuum Travel Sports Gym Hiking Camping
Better
Insulated Water Bottle 32 oz Stainless Steel

Move supporting details into the description, structured fields, variants, filters, or metafields. Those places can carry more context without making the title harder to read.

4. A simple title formula

Use this as a starting point, not a rigid template:

Product type + key attribute + important qualifier

5. Quick checklist before publishing

  • Can a shopper tell what the product is at a glance?
  • Does the title include the key attribute that helps it stand out?
  • Is every detail accurate and specific?
  • Is it easy to scan on mobile?
  • Did you leave nonessential details for the rest of the product page?
