Use Google Search Console Queries in Your Shopify Listing Workflow

Bring available product-page searches into your next draft, with product facts and your judgment guiding the final wording.

By the penstack team
October 15, 2026 · 5 min read

A useful phrase can already be in your store’s search data.

Penstack lets you connect Google Search Console, review reported queries for a product page, and choose relevant phrases before generating a description. You keep the product facts and the final review in the same workflow.

The short answer

Connect the matching storefront property, choose a product, select phrases that fit its verified facts, then generate and review. Google is optional; you can keep working without search data.

Connect the property for the store you are editing.

Open Manage store in Penstack and find Google Search Console. Connect the Google account with access to your storefront’s property. Authorize read-only access, then choose the property that matches that storefront.

If the property is missing, check which Google account has access. Use Refresh properties after adding access, or Change Google account if needed. For multiple stores, manage the connection and property for each store separately.

Read the queries in the context of one product.

Choose a product with a public Shopify storefront page. Under Shape your content, the Search queries for this product section shows available reported phrases, impressions, and clicks for that page.

Penstack requests finalized web-search data for a 90-day window ending three days before today. The displayed dates, property, page URL, and retrieval time identify the source. A newer page can use whatever data is available in that window.

  • Impressions: appearances of this product page in search results.
  • Clicks: visits to the page from those results.

These counts describe the product page’s reported visibility. They are not total keyword search volume, and Google does not report every query.

Choose relevance before popularity.

Select up to five phrases that accurately describe the product before choosing Generate Description. A phrase can help with wording without proving a product claim.

For example, “blue snowboard” can fit a board with a verified blue design. A query for “beginner snowboard” does not establish rider suitability. Skip any phrase that needs facts you do not have.

Your selected phrases are sent to Anthropic with the product information for that generation request. Read the Google data disclosure for the processing and connection details.

Review the draft, then choose what to publish.

Selected queries guide the next draft. Check its wording against the product facts, make your edits, and review the listing before publishing to Shopify. Selecting a phrase does not publish it.

Keep useful existing content. Search guidance is another source of wording direction, not a reason to rewrite every field or add unsupported claims. Use the listing-refresh guide to decide what to keep and what to change.

Keep working when there is little or no search data.

A working Google connection can return few queries or none. Confirm the storefront property and public product page. A product without a public URL cannot supply product-page query guidance.

When no queries are reported, generate from product facts. You do not need to wait for 90 days of history to use available data. If access needs attention, reconnect from Manage store. See connection and data questions.

Use this for draft guidance.

Google Search Console guidance is optional and included across Penstack plans within your existing publishing allowance. It helps bring available search phrases into listing work.

This integration does not run SEO A/B tests or establish that a wording change caused more traffic. Its search counts are source context for your draft. Keep any assessment of later performance separate from the drafting example.
