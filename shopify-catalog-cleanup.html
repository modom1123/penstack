WHEN THE CATALOG OUTGROWS ITS COPY

The gaps become the shopper’s work.

As products accumulate, the same questions can go unanswered across dozens of listings. Start with the gaps that make choosing harder.

Descriptions that could fit anything

“Great for everyday use” leaves the shopper guessing about size, materials, and the reason to choose this product.

Photos without useful context

Repeated or missing alt text can leave different product views with the same description.

Organization that changes by listing

Inconsistent tags and collection assignments make related products harder to keep together.

A FOCUSED CLEANUP SESSION

Improve one listing. Establish a standard.

penstack brings product copy and supporting details into one working session. Use the first product to define what the next listing needs.

  1. Choose the gap to fix

    Select an existing Shopify product. Decide whether its biggest gap is vague copy, missing image descriptions, or product organization.

  2. Bring in the facts

    Use the Collection Brief, existing Product Description, and product metafields. Confirm materials, dimensions, care, and intended use where relevant.

  3. Prepare and review the updates

    Generate the draft. Check the description, supported details, search wording, image text, tags, and collections against your source information.

  4. Publish the selected changes

    Send the reviewed updates to the same Shopify product. Apply the same checks as you work through the next listing.

A WORKED EXAMPLE

Replace vague praise with an answer.

This illustrative example uses a ceramic mug with confirmed facts: 400 ml capacity, stoneware construction, and dishwasher-safe care.

BEFORE

“Your new favorite mug.”

“A beautiful mug, perfect for everyday use. A great addition to any kitchen.”

The shopper still needs to know its capacity, material, and care.

AFTER REVIEW

“A 400 ml stoneware mug for your daily coffee.”

“Room for a generous cup, with stoneware construction and dishwasher-safe care for an easy daily routine.”

The description answers what it holds, what it is made from, and how to care for it.

A sample of the intended result, rather than a customer outcome. Your product facts determine what belongs in your draft.

BEFORE YOU START

Know what the first session includes.

Can I use penstack on existing Shopify products?

Yes. Select an existing product and prepare the content changes you need. Review the selected updates before publishing them to Shopify.

Will it clean up my entire catalog automatically?

The workflow is built around selected products and reviewed updates. This page describes content cleanup, rather than a full catalog scan or automatic repair of duplicate products, variants, or inventory.

What can I try on Free?

Free includes the core product workflow for up to 10 product publishes per calendar month, one connected store, and one team member. Brand Voice is available on Solo, Pro, and Agency.
