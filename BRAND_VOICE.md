# penstack voice and tone

penstack is a curious, clear guide to Shopify product content and SEO. Explain the mechanism, make it concrete, and leave the reader with something useful to do.

## Writing rules

- Lead with the customer’s task. Name the product, field, or action involved.
- Explain unfamiliar terms with a practical example. Search intent is the need behind a query; show the need with a specific product search.
- Use short sentences and ordinary words. Let precision carry the authority.
- Use light wit when it clarifies the work. Keep metaphors brief and relevant.
- Describe what penstack actually does. Identify the inputs, the draft, the review, and the publish step.
- State plan access precisely. Brand Voice starts at Solo; multiple stores start at Pro.
- Treat generated text as a draft. The merchant checks facts and approves publishing.
- Make comparisons fair. Cite current primary documentation and acknowledge integrations or differences between vendors.
- Use lowercase `penstack`. Use `Brand Voice` for the named feature. Preserve actual app labels such as `Collection Brief`, `Status`, and `Item`.

## Page responsibilities

| Page | Main question |
| --- | --- |
| Home | What does penstack help me finish? |
| Product | What happens to my product at each step? |
| Use Cases | Where does this help with my store’s current problem? |
| Compare | Which approach fits the work I need done? |
| Pricing | Which allowance, team size, and store limit fit my operation? |

## Examples

Prefer “Choose a Shopify product, draft its description, and check the details before publishing” to “Unlock an integrated product-content workflow.”

Prefer “A shopper searching for a dishwasher-safe ceramic mug has told you what matters” to “Leverage long-tail SEO strategy implementation.”

Prefer “Solo adds Brand Voice and more monthly capacity for one store” to “Paid plans unlock increasing operating leverage.”

## Claims to avoid

- Guaranteed rankings, sales, growth, or time savings without evidence.
- Unsupported setup durations, universal field support, or automatic approvals.
- Suggesting penstack performs independent keyword research when describing supplied search context.
- Describing an upcoming feature or old screenshot as the current release.
- Blanket claims about competitor limitations when integrations can change the result.
- Internal editing notes in customer-facing copy.

## Publishing check

Read each page on its own. Confirm that its headline, body, metadata, and CTA serve its assigned question. Check current app behavior and plan definitions. Review desktop and mobile wrapping after copy edits.
