PUT SEARCH INTENT TO WORK

A search phrase is a clue about what to explain.

“Ceramic mug” names a category. “Dishwasher-safe ceramic mug” adds a practical requirement. If the product supports that detail, make it easy to find in the listing. penstack helps apply the context you provide as you prepare product content.

1. Read the intentChoose relevant phrases that tell you what the shopper needs to know.
2. Explain the productUse verified details to answer that need in readable copy.
3. Describe the imagesWrite alt text about the actual image. Keep names useful and descriptive.
4. Organize the itemAssign tags and collections that fit the product and your catalog.
5. Check the resultRead the listing for accuracy and clarity before publishing.
