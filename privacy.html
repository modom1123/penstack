PRIVACY POLICY

How penstack handles information.

This policy explains the information penstack processes when you use the service, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Effective date: September 16, 2026

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use penstack, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: information used to create and manage your penstack account, such as your name, email address, authentication information, plan, and account preferences.
  • Connected store information: store identifiers, authorization details, and store information needed to connect penstack to a store you authorize.
  • Product and catalog information: product titles, descriptions, images and image metadata, product details, tags, collections, custom fields, SEO-related content, and other product information you choose to retrieve, edit, generate, or publish through penstack.
  • Support communications: information you provide when you contact support, including messages and troubleshooting details.
  • Technical and usage information: information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service, such as device, browser, log, error, and usage information.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and operate penstack, authenticate users, connect authorized stores, retrieve and publish product information, generate and organize product content, provide support, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, administer plans and usage, comply with legal obligations, and improve the service.

3. Connected store data

penstack accesses store and product information only after a merchant authorizes the connection. Access is limited to the permissions granted to the app and is used to provide the product workflow requested by the merchant. penstack does not require access to customer order data to provide its core product-content workflow unless that functionality is added and separately disclosed in the future.

4. AI-assisted features

Some penstack features use automated or AI-assisted systems to help create, transform, organize, or review product content. Information needed to perform those requests may be processed by service providers that support penstack’s operation. Users are responsible for reviewing generated content before publishing it.

5. Service providers

We may use service providers to host infrastructure, provide authentication, deliver email, process app billing, monitor service performance, provide AI-assisted functionality, and support other operational needs. These providers process information on penstack’s behalf or as otherwise described by their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. When information may be shared

We do not sell merchant personal information. We may share information with service providers as necessary to operate penstack, when a user directs us to do so, when required by law or legal process, to protect the rights and security of penstack or others, or as part of a business transaction such as a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the service. When a store connection or account is removed, information is deleted or retained in accordance with applicable obligations and operational requirements.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your penstack account, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Merchants may disconnect a store or uninstall the app through Shopify. Privacy rights may vary depending on your location.

10. International processing

penstack and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you are located. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for applicable cross-border data transfers.

11. Children

penstack is intended for businesses and is not directed to children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as penstack changes. We will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date when material changes are made.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@penstack.co.
