# penstack illustration system

Core marketing artwork uses navy outlines, a warm paper background, and restrained green, blue, and yellow accents. Text, headings, labels, and explanations belong in HTML using the shared DM Sans typography. Illustrations explain a concept; they are not screenshots or evidence of automatic fact checking.

## Color roles

- Navy: structure, outlines, and readable text.
- Blue and pale blue: product facts, inputs, and catalog context.
- Green and pale green: approved changes and completion.
- Yellow and pale yellow: human review and attention.
- Product colors can use the brand palette without implying a status.

Do not restore the old beginner/experienced/expert color mapping. Do not introduce decorative pink or purple blocks. Keep the same product in every part of one example.

Originals are 1672 × 941. Web delivery uses 1280 × 720 WebP derivatives in assets/illustrations, with original PNG fallbacks. Preserve the originals. Use a picture element, explicit image dimensions, and descriptive alt text. Load below-fold illustrations lazily. Do not crop the artwork into a different shape or add text inside the PNG. Shared styles live in illustrations.css.

Icons use 24px SVG view boxes, 1.7px navy strokes, and rounded line ends. Hide purely decorative icons from assistive technology. Use the same drawing grammar in HTML workflow diagrams. Meaning must remain clear without color alone.

## Verification

Check that each product matches its section. Review all examples at their actual desktop and mobile display sizes. Check captions, alt text, image loading, and spacing. Confirm that review is a human step and that no art promises ranking improvements or automatic validation. Blog artwork remains a separate follow-up pass.
