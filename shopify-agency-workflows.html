Adding stores brings more context to manage: which product belongs to which client, which voice applies, and how much publishing capacity remains.

Different audiences

A skincare store and a snowboard store need different language, product facts, and search context.

More people in the work

A shared workspace needs intentional team access. Invite the people who need to contribute and keep membership current.

Uneven workloads

One store may have a launch while another needs a small refresh. Pro and Agency pool the monthly product allowance across connected stores.

THE MULTI-STORE WORKFLOW

Make context part of every session.

Keep a repeatable review process while giving each store its own product information and expression.

  1. Connect the client’s store

    Choose Pro or Agency for multiple Shopify stores. Confirm the selected store before starting its product work.

  2. Assign its Brand Voice

    Create or choose the voice intended for that store. Each store has one active voice; draft voices can remain in the library.

  3. Work from its own context

    Prepare the listing from that store’s product facts and SEO context. Check the draft against the client’s voice and the product source information.

  4. Publish and check capacity

    Publish the reviewed changes to the selected store. Check shared usage and connected-store counts as you plan the next session.

BEFORE YOU BRING CLIENT STORES IN

Set the boundaries once. Check them as you work.

Does every connected store use the same Brand Voice?

No. Each store has its own active Brand Voice assignment. Pro and Agency also keep SEO context separate by store.

How does the pooled product allowance work?

The workspace’s connected stores share the monthly product allowance. It is 400 on Pro and 1,500 on Agency, rather than a separate allowance of that size for each store.

Can I add people to the workspace?

Pro supports up to five team members and Agency supports up to eight. Manage invitations and team access from My Account.

What should I prepare before onboarding a client?

Have the Shopify store connection, reliable product information, and an agreed voice direction ready. Choose a first product and define the checks it must pass before publication. Read the agency onboarding guide →
