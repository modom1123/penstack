HOW IT WORKS

Retrieve → Generate → Review → Publish

The workflow starts with a product already in Shopify. A draft product works too; you do not need to publish it first.

1

Retrieve

Choose the product’s status and item. Retrieve its existing description, images, and available product fields.

2

Generate

Confirm the Collection Brief, Product Description, and source details. On paid plans, apply the store’s Brand Voice.

3

Review

Read the draft beside the product inputs. Check names, claims, practical details, and image descriptions.

4

Publish

Publish the selected updates to the same Shopify product. Successful publishes are recorded in your usage.

WHAT YOU CAN WORK ON

Different fields answer different shopper questions.

A description explains the product. Image text explains a photo. Structured details make practical information easier to find. Each deserves its own content.

Product descriptions

Draft from the source information you supply, then check the wording against the actual product.

SEO + discovery

Prepare a search title and meta description using relevant product context. Preview the wording before publishing.

Images + alt text

Work with existing product photos. Use color, view, and shot type to prepare image titles and descriptive alt text.

Product details

Add verified features and care information. Edit supported plain-text metafields; manage structured field types in Shopify.

Tags + collections

Review product tags and collection assignments against the way your catalog is organized.

Brand voice

Solo, Pro, and Agency include the ten-question quiz. Pro and Agency assign an active voice to each connected store.

Review + approval

Check the draft, search preview, image text, and supported fields together before the Shopify handoff.

Publish to Shopify

Update the selected product. Normal edits to the same product do not use another publish; repurposing it can.

THE INPUTS SHAPE THE DRAFT

Useful source details make useful copy.

The Collection Brief gives the wider context. The Product Description and metafields supply product-level detail. Brand Voice guides expression; it does not establish whether a product claim is true.

WHAT REVIEW MEANS

Check meaning, not just spelling.

A fluent sentence can still contain an unsupported claim. Compare the draft with your source facts, check the images, and confirm the product is organized correctly.

Use a listing review checklist →

✓Claims match the source facts.
✓Image descriptions match the actual view.
✓Tags and collections fit the product.
