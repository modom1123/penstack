How usage works

Count publishing work, not the number of drafts you generate.
Published productsA previously uncounted product uses one publish after a successful Shopify update. Generating a draft does not use a publish.
Edits + republishesNormal edits to the same product do not use another publish. Repurposing the record for a materially different product can count again.
Monthly resetFree resets by calendar month. Paid allowances follow the subscription’s usage period. Unused capacity does not roll over.
Pooled plansPro and Agency share capacity across connected stores.

HOW POOLED CAPACITY WORKS

Share the allowance where the work is.

On Pro, 250 publishes for one store and 100 for another use 350 of the 400-product allowance. The remaining 50 can go to any connected store; capacity is not divided into fixed store quotas.

Separate Brand Voice by storeThe allowance is shared. Each store still has its own active Brand Voice assignment.
Separate SEO context by storeKeep the product facts, search direction, and collections relevant to the selected store.
One shared allowanceStore and team limits still apply, even when publishing capacity remains.
Drafts are not publishes.

For example, first publishing 18 products and making 50 normal edits to those same products uses 18 publishes, not 68.

Annual billing still has usage periods.

Annual payment does not turn the monthly allowance into one yearly pool. Check the current usage period in My Account.
