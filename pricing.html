How usage works

Simple, predictable limits. No surprise product-count math.
Published productsA product counts only after a successful publish to Shopify.
Edits + republishesNormal edits to the same product do not consume another product.
Monthly resetAllowances reset monthly; unused capacity does not roll over.
Pooled plansPro and Agency share capacity across connected stores.
