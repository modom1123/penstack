A product only counts when it successfully publishes to Shopify. Failed attempts do not count. Normal edits and republishes of the same product—including copy, SEO, tags, and image tests—do not use another product. Turning an existing listing into a materially different product counts as a new product after you confirm the replacement.
When your allowance resets. Free resets each calendar month. Paid plans receive a fresh monthly allowance, whether you pay monthly or annually. Monthly subscriptions reset with their Shopify payment cycle; annual subscriptions reset on monthly anniversaries of the annual cycle start. Unused capacity does not roll over. Pro and Agency share the allowance across connected stores.

WHY STORE CONTEXT MATTERS

Each store needs its own context.

A skincare store and an outdoor store speak to different needs. Pro and Agency keep each store’s Brand Voice and SEO context separate while sharing one product allowance.

Separate Brand Voice by storeApply the voice and writing preferences intended for each connected store.
Separate SEO context by storeUse the search direction and collection context relevant to that store’s products.
One shared allowanceUse the pooled capacity where it is needed. There is no fixed split between stores.
