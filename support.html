SUPPORT

Need a hand?

If something in penstack is not working as expected, send us the details and we’ll help you figure out what happened.

Email supportsupport@penstack.co

What we can help with

Account accessSign-in, password recovery, or account questions.
Store connectionsConnecting, reconnecting, or managing an authorized store.
Product retrievalProblems fetching an existing product into penstack.
Product contentQuestions about generated or editable product content and fields.
PublishingPublishing requirements, errors, or content that did not update as expected.
BillingQuestions about plans, usage, or charges.

What to include

To help us troubleshoot quickly, include the email address associated with your penstack account, the store you were working with, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and any error message you saw. Screenshots are helpful when they do not contain sensitive information.

Protect your account

Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys, payment card details, or other secrets by email. penstack support will never ask you to send your password.

Contact

Email support@penstack.co for product and account support.
