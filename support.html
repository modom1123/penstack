SUPPORT

Find the next step when something gets stuck.

Use the checks below to narrow down the problem. If it continues, send the details to support so we can investigate the right step.

Email supportsupport@penstack.co

Start with the step that stopped working

Account accessUse “Forgot password?” on the login screen. For a new account, confirm the email before connecting Shopify.
Store connectionsCheck which Shopify store is selected and whether its connection is active. Use the app’s connection controls to reconnect it.
Product retrievalConfirm the product exists in the selected Shopify store. Choose its Active or Draft status, then select the item.
Product contentCheck the Collection Brief, Product Description, and source details. Include which field or claim needs attention.
PublishingCheck the selected product in Shopify to see what changed. Include the field that did not update and the exact error message.
BillingCheck the plan and usage shown in My Account. See what counts as a publish before reporting an unexpected allowance change.

Send a report we can act on

Include your account email, store, product if relevant, and the step you were completing. Describe the expected result and actual result, then add the error text and approximate time. Screenshots help show the state of the screen.

Protect your account

Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys, payment card details, or other secrets by email. penstack support will never ask you to send your password.

Need instructions rather than troubleshooting?

Open the app’s walkthrough for screen-by-screen help. For listing skills and catalog methods, browse the guides. Email support@penstack.co when you need us to investigate.
