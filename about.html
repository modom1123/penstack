OUR STORY

Created out of frustration, born out of experience.

Our founder has spent years across product, marketing, ecommerce, onboarding, analytics, and customer experience. Again and again, the same pattern showed up: the strategic work was interesting, but getting products online required too much repetitive work spread across too many places.

The time-consuming nature of product listing did more than eat hours. It drained the energy needed for strategy. By the time descriptions, product details, images, alt text, organization, and publishing were finished, there was often very little enthusiasm left except to be done with it.

THE IDEA BEHIND PENSTACK

“Getting a product online is one repetitive task after another. There had to be a better way.”

That frustration became the starting point for Penstack: a product workflow that handles the repetitive middle without removing the person who knows the product, the customer, and the brand.

MORE TIME FOR WHAT MATTERS

Repetitive work drains creativity and focus.

Manually writing product descriptions, image alt text, image titles, custom fields, tags, collections, and then checking and publishing everything can eat up hours and days that could be spent on strategy, brand, customer experience, merchandising, and growth.

Penstack takes the repetitive work off your plate.

It gives you a head start on the fields that consume time and keeps the workflow together so your attention is not constantly bouncing between tools.

You still make the decisions.

Penstack is not about outsourcing judgment to AI. You review, edit, refine, organize, and decide when the product is ready to publish.

A SMALL BUT REAL EXAMPLE

Alt text often just needs a useful starting point.

Writing image alt text can feel like another item on a long list. In many cases, the useful thing is not a final answer handed to you. It is a clear, descriptive starting point that is faster to adjust than staring at an empty field. That is what Penstack is designed to provide.

Starting from nothing

Open each image, think through what matters, write the first draft, edit it, repeat for every image, and hope there is still time left for the rest of the product.

Starting with Penstack

Get a descriptive draft based on the product context, adjust it where the human judgment matters, and keep moving through the product without losing the thread.
